About

0xCigar is the handle I write under here. This site is where I put down red team and blue team engagement notes, CTF solves, bug bounty research, and the study notes from whatever certification I'm working through at the time — mostly so I have an accurate record to look back on, and so anyone evaluating my work can see it directly rather than taking my word for it.

I split my time roughly evenly between offense and defense: breaking things during authorized assessments, and building the detection logic that would have caught me doing it. Writeups here are grouped that way on purpose — red team and blue team aren't opposing camps to me, they're two ends of the same problem.

Writeups exist because learning in public builds a track record that a resume can't — anyone can claim a skill, but a documented methodology, a real detection rule, or a tool that's seen actual use says more than a line item.

For my formal CV, certifications, and professional contact details, see ayushwahane.com.

Current focus

  • Certifications in progress: CCNA, Security+, and CPTS — notes for each are on the Notes page as I go, not after the fact.
  • Active on: TryHackMe, HackTheBox, PortSwigger's Web Security Academy, and Proving Grounds.

How this site is organized

  • Writeups — engagement notes and CTF solves, grouped red team / blue team.
  • Research & OSS — disclosures, maintained tools, and upstream contributions.
  • Notes — certification study notes, grouped by cert.
  • Projects — tools built out of engagement or research necessity.
